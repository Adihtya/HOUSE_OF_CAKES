# Cafe UI Design Skill

## Visual Direction
1. **Warmth & Sensuality**: Combine tropical warmth (mango, passion fruit, papaya, hibiscus) with French patisserie precision (glossy ganache, delicate piping, gold leaf accents).
2. **Color Balance**: Use Warm Cream (`#FFF4E5`) as the unifying canvas. Punctuate with Deep Cherry (`#8F1235`), Raspberry Pink (`#F13D70`), Tropical Orange (`#FF7448`), and Leaf Green (`#456B4A`).
3. **Typography**: Pair Cormorant Garamond (high-contrast, editorial elegance, italic flourishes) with DM Sans (clean, highly legible UI labels, numbers, and navigation).
4. **Art Direction**:
   - Plating and presentation should look mouthwatering, with realistic shadows and three-dimensional depth.
   - Editorial asymmetry: break rigid grids with floating fruit badges, numbered cake tags, and layered cards.
   - Micro-details: subtle borders, organic pill shapes, frosted glass surfaces, and warm highlights.
